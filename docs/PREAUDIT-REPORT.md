## Executive summary

The QuoPull core contracts are in good shape. Across all six review dimensions there are NO high/critical code vulnerabilities and NO attacker-triggerable loss-of-funds paths. The draw/claim/vault core, the tax-hook logic, the convert split accounting, and the mint/allowlist/delegate flows are sound: fund flows are correctly bounded, nonReentrant, and vault-scoped, and the convert() pipeline is atomic and keeper-gated. Every genuinely material risk that remains is a DEPLOY / LAUNCH-ARTIFACT gap, not a contract logic bug. The two medium findings both live in the go-live process and both falsify a documented guarantee: (1) the convert MEV/donation-brick caps (maxConvertPerCall / maxWethConvertPerCall) ship fail-OPEN at type(uint256).max and are wired by NO deploy or go-live script (setters appear only in tests), which nullifies audit fixes H-1/H-3 and the code's own stated "bounded convert MEV" invariant; and (2) there is no reviewed mainnet go-live script that routes LP through the permanent QpullLiquidityLock (only GoLiveTestnet.s.sol references it, and Deploy.s.sol's printed steps say "initialize + add LP" with no lock), so the central "can't rug" promise rests entirely on manual operator discipline and the printed path actively points at the removable position. Note: the two review dimensions surfaced the convert-cap gap twice (treasury-convert medium + access-failsafe low); it is ONE issue, taken at medium. Both mediums are cheap to close because the correct primitives (the cap setters, the lock) already exist and work. The remaining lows are latent config-fragility footguns and narrow key-loss / liveness edges (all owner- or permissionlessly-recoverable, none with fund loss); the infos are accurate disclosures of intentional trust trade-offs plus documentation drift. Biggest risk = the launch/deploy process, not the code: the "can't rug" and "bounded MEV" stories are true in the primitives but not enforced by any reviewed mainnet artifact. Recommendation: close the two mediums, make the deploy wire fail-closed, clean the doc drift, then hand to external audit; the contract code itself is essentially audit-ready.

### [MEDIUM] convert() MEV/donation-brick caps (maxConvertPerCall, maxWethConvertPerCall) ship uncapped and are never wired at deploy

- **Dimension:** treasury-convert
- **Location:** script/Deploy.s.sol:265-272; defaults src/Treasury.sol:48,55; checklist Deploy.s.sol:320-324

**Reasoning:** Reproduced from code. Both caps default to type(uint256).max (Treasury.sol:48,55). _wire (Deploy.s.sol:265-272) sets adapters (265), routing (268), calls lockRouting (270), then setConvertThreshold (271) and setKeeper (272) — it NEVER calls setMaxConvertPerCall/setMaxWethConvertPerCall. lockRouting() (Treasury.sol:132-141) requires only adapters + the four destinations, not the caps, so it freezes routing with both caps still at max. The go-live checklist in _log (320-324) lists only pool initialize + add LP; no cap step. A repo-wide grep shows the two setters are called ONLY in test/TreasuryFlow.t.sol and test/InvariantTreasurySplit.t.sol — no script/*.s.sol sets them. So at launch the caps are max until a manual, unenforced operator step. This directly defeats the documented H-1 (WETH-donation brick of leg 2) and H-3 (QPULL-donation brick of leg 1) fixes and falsifies the code's own stated invariant of 'bounded, cap-limited convert MEV' (Treasury.sol:60-63). Scenario (a): a large WETH donation makes wethHeld huge; convert() would push 80% through the shallow QUOTRON pool in one router.buyExactEth (Treasury.sol:218-227) — the honest keeper's quoteConvert() sizes prizeWeth off the full uncapped balance (keeper.js:290-296) and either its quote reverts (returns null → skip) or the onchain swap reverts, and because convert() is nonReentrant+atomic the QPULL leg is rolled back too; net effect is a funding-pipeline stall until the owner sets a cap. Scenario (b): a leaked keeper key can call convert(1,1) and sandwich the ENTIRE accumulated QPULL+WETH balance through both pools in a single unbounded call; the caps are the only per-slice bound that would have limited a compromised-keeper sandwich to one pool-sized slice. Both are owner-recoverable (the cap setters are intentionally not frozen by lockRouting, per L-6), but the launch window is genuinely exposed and the reliance on memory is the exact silent-vulnerable-default the caps were added to remove. Severity medium is correct: conditional (needs a donation grief or key compromise) and recoverable, but it nullifies two documented audit fixes and a stated MEV bound with zero onchain enforcement.

**Fix:** Wire both caps fail-closed in Deploy (or at go-live, since they are pool-sized and the QPULL/WETH pool only exists at launch): add MAX_CONVERT_PER_CALL / MAX_WETH_CONVERT_PER_CALL to _cfg via vm.envUint (like rafflePotCap/leaderboardPotCap at Deploy.s.sol:120-121, which fail the deploy closed if unset) and call setMaxConvertPerCall/setMaxWethConvertPerCall in _wire BEFORE lockRouting; OR make lockRouting() revert unless both caps are non-max; OR at minimum add an explicit 'set both convert caps to pool-sized values' line to the go-live checklist in _log (Deploy.s.sol:320-324). Enforcing onchain removes the silent default.

**Resolution:** CLOSED — `src/Treasury.sol` (both caps default `0` = not configured; `convert()` reverts `NotConfigured` until both are armed) + `script/GoLiveMainnet.s.sol` (step 1 arms both caps pool-sized and asserts `!= 0 && != type(uint256).max` before `lockRouting()`); `LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md` pinned gate E + §6b step 2.

**Reasoning:** Confirmed. Treasury.sol:48 (`maxConvertPerCall = type(uint256).max`) and :55 (`maxWethConvertPerCall = type(uint256).max`) both default fully open. Deploy.s.sol._wire (224-281) wires setAdapters, setRouting, lockRouting, setConvertThreshold and setKeeper but never calls setMaxConvertPerCall/setMaxWethConvertPerCall -- and it cannot meaningfully size them pre-pool. Better: change the storage defaults at Treasury.sol:48,55 from type(uint256).max to 0 and have convert() treat 0 as 'not configured' (revert NotConfigured) so the H-1/H-3 protection can never be silently left off; or add a one-time owner arm step scripted immediately after go-live. Note the setters already reject 0 (setMaxConvertPerCall/setMaxWethConvertPerCall revert BelowThreshold on 0), so using 0 as the sentinel is consistent.

**Resolution:** CLOSED — `src/Treasury.sol` (storage defaults changed from `type(uint256).max` to `0`; `convert()` treats `0` as `NotConfigured`; the setters still reject `0`), armed + asserted by `script/GoLiveMainnet.s.sol`; `LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md` pinned gate E. Same issue as the treasury-convert MEDIUM above.

### [LOW] Engine `genesis` silently defaults to Deploy time, which can start the 48h accumulation + first-raffle clocks before go-live

- **Dimension:** access-failsafe-deploy
- **Location:** script/Deploy.s.sol:98; src/RaffleEngine.sol:181-182,204,213; LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md:48

**Reasoning:** Confirmed. Deploy.s.sol:98 sets `e.genesis = vm.envOr("GENESIS", block.timestamp)` -- defaulting genesis to the moment Deploy runs, which is a distinct step that runs after the NFT raise and can precede go-live (initialize + LP) by hours or days. RaffleEngine measures its schedule from genesis: currentDay() = (block.timestamp - genesis)/DAY() (RaffleEngine.sol:181-182); runOpeningDraw gates on `currentDay() < ACCUM_DAYS` i.e. genesis+48h (line 204); the opening beacon round is `drawRound(ACCUM_DAYS-1)` off genesis (line 177,213). launchTime is stamped separately in the hook at initialize, NOT genesis. So if Deploy runs >48h before go-live with GENESIS unset, at go-live currentDay() already >= ACCUM_DAYS and the permissionless runOpeningDraw can fire immediately on a pool that had no trading (hence no pack cohorts) during [0, ACCUM_DAYS-1], collapsing the intended 48h opening-accumulation window. The wiring guard at Deploy.s.sol:258-261 only asserts HolderDraw.genesis()==Raffle.genesis() (internal consistency), not that genesis==go-live, so a deploy-time value passes. Severity low is correct: no fund loss -- the empty-window case is handled (RaffleEngine.sol:217-225 sets openingDone and rolls pots forward), and the pot itself is convert()-funded so it is near-empty too; the damage is a silently desynced launch cadence/fairness. It is a genuine fail-open footgun where the sibling pot configs fail closed, and although LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md:48 documents pinning genesis, the default itself is the hazard.

**Fix:** Make GENESIS a required env var: change Deploy.s.sol:98 to `e.genesis = vm.envUint("GENESIS")` so an unset value fails the deploy closed (matching the MIN_POT/POT_CAP fail-closed pattern at lines 114-121) and forces a deliberate value pinned to the go-live timestamp. Alternatively, derive the accumulation/first-raffle clock from the hook's launchTime (stamped at initialize) rather than a deploy-time genesis, so the schedule cannot start before trading is possible.

**Resolution:** CLOSED — `script/Deploy.s.sol` (`e.genesis = vm.envUint("GENESIS")`, fail-closed; the `block.timestamp` default is gone); `LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md` pinned gate E + §6b step 1 (pin to the go-live timestamp, read back equal on all four genesis-bearing contracts).

### [LOW] Opening-draw small-pot branch leaves openingDone=false, blocking ALL daily draws until the vault clears minPot

- **Dimension:** games-claims
- **Location:** src/RaffleEngine.sol:208-211 (gate at :232; contrast :222-226)

**Reasoning:** Reproduced from code. runOpeningDraw()'s small-pot guard at lines 208-211 `if (pot < minPot || (pot*bucketBps(0))/BPS < OPENING_WINNERS) { emit ...; return; }` returns BEFORE line 222 `openingDone = true;`. runDraw() at line 232 hard-gates every daily draw on `if (!openingDone) revert OpeningPending();`. So while vault.freeBalance() stays below minPot at/after the 48h window close, the opening cannot settle and no daily draw can run. This is exactly the asymmetry described: the empty-window branch (223-226) DOES set openingDone (deliberately, per the 217-221 comment), while the small-pot branch does not. Mitigating facts lowering severity: (1) runOpeningDraw() is permissionless (line 202, only nonReentrant), so anyone can retry the instant the vault is funded, unlocking daily draws in one tx; (2) even if daily draws were unlocked, runDraw() applies the SAME `pot < minPot` void at line 245, so a daily draw during the unfunded period would void anyway with no payout and no tickets burned. The genuine incremental harm is narrow: cohorts whose single draw-day window (currentDay()==day+1) elapses in the gap between the vault crossing minPot and someone calling runOpeningDraw void their shots. Value is never lost (pot rolls forward). This is a real, recoverable, conditional liveness/consistency issue, correctly rated low.

**Fix:** Two options. (b), lowest-risk: treat 'vault.freeBalance() must exceed minPot before the ACCUM_DAYS window closes' as a documented hard launch invariant and keep minPot conservative at launch (minPot defaults to 0 and MUST be set per runbook, so this is a config discipline). (a), code-level: settle openingDone=true even on the small-pot path so daily draws unlock (they self-void under minPot until funded); but note that literally returning early after setting openingDone would strand cohorts [0,ACCUM_DAYS-1] (only the line 216 sweep ever pops them). The clean version is to ALWAYS run drawFromPaidOnly to sweep [0,ACCUM_DAYS-1] and set openingDone once currentDay()>=ACCUM_DAYS, paying nobody when the pot is too small (the pot rolls forward) — mirroring the empty-window reasoning at 217-221.

**Resolution:** CLOSED (decision 1a) — `src/RaffleEngine.sol`: the small-pot path now settles `openingDone = true` and pays nobody; daily draws unlock and self-void under `minPot`. The forgone opening burns no tickets and reads no beacon (no `drawFromPaidOnly` sweep: cohorts `[0, ACCUM_DAYS-1]` are already inside `runDraw(ACCUM_DAYS)`'s rolling paid window, so the "stranded cohorts" caveat in the Fix text above does not apply), and it is reachable only after the `Accumulating` guard, so a payable opening (pot >= `minPot`) still takes the unchanged paid path. Void-on-miss posture and the runbook expectation (fund the vault above `minPot` before the window closes) documented in NatSpec; covered by the three `test_L_opening*` tests in `test/RaffleFlow.t.sol`.

### [LOW] 7-day rip-XP claim window lets a holder choose between two leaderboard weeks, contradicting the documented M-2 'cannot choose which week' guarantee

- **Dimension:** games-claims
- **Location:** src/PackRegistry.sol:602-609 (RIP_XP_WINDOW=7 at :67); LeaderboardRegistry.sol:97-105,147-150

**Reasoning:** Reproduced. isRipXpClaimable() (605-607) makes a settled non-winner claimable for `t in {settled+1 .. settled+7}` where settled = cohortDay+LIFE_DAYS+1 and t=_today() — 7 consecutive genesis-days = a 7-wall-clock-day claim window. claimRipXp() (line 628) calls leaderboard.recordXp, and LeaderboardRegistry._addPoints books into `_week()` computed at CALL time (line 99, week = (block.timestamp - weekAnchor)/WEEK, WEEK=7 days, Sunday-aligned). A half-open interval of length exactly 7 days contains exactly one Sunday week boundary (interval length equals week spacing), so a holder claiming before that Sunday banks into week W and on/after it into week W+1 — a genuine binary choice. The M-2 comment (66-67) 'the holder cannot choose which leaderboard week to bank it into' therefore overstates: a 7-day window over 7-day weeks yields a two-week choice, not zero choice. No guard prevents this (single claim, but the holder picks WHEN). Practical impact is negligible as the finding states: RIP_XP_UNIT=1e15 (x1..x10) is tiny next to buy points (gross QPULL, ~1e18+ scale), so the timing choice barely moves top-25 standings; no funds at risk. Genuine invariant/comment mismatch with a real but minor gameable choice — low (info is also defensible given the negligible material impact).

**Fix:** If M-2 is meant to be exact, bank rip XP into the leaderboard week that contains the pack's settlement instant (genesis + settled*DAY) rather than the caller's claim day — add a LeaderboardRegistry entrypoint that credits a caller-independent target week and have claimRipXp compute it. Or shorten RIP_XP_WINDOW below one week so the window cannot straddle a boundary. Otherwise, correct the M-2 comment to state the window reduces the choice to at most two adjacent weeks (not eliminates it).

**Resolution:** ACCEPTED-RESIDUAL — `SECURITY.md` §16.7. The M-2 comment in `src/PackRegistry.sol` is corrected (the window BOUNDS the choice to two adjacent weeks); the code is deliberately unchanged, because crediting the settlement week would silently lose XP claimed after that week's `distribute()`. Residual negligible (`RIP_XP_UNIT` 1e15 vs ~1e18 buy points).

### [LOW] Pledge-flood can void most virtual free-entry seats and deny NFT holders their ~10% allocation (per-seat reject cap does not 'kill the flood vector')

- **Dimension:** games-claims
- **Location:** src/PackRegistry.sol:475-495 (_pickVirtual); :470 (wb); :482 shared budget; comments :112,:130-131

**Reasoning:** Reproduced. _buildFreeCfg line 470 sets `cfg.wb = cfg.srCount*SR_WEIGHT + pledgeList[drawDay].length` — the FULL weight-expanded pledge list, which is never pruned on transfer (pledgeList is append-only; pledgerOf/ownerSince are only checked at draw). _buildSrList (435-448) filters SR to valid holders so srWeightTotal reflects only valid SR, but the denominator wb is inflated by INVALID pledges. In _pickVirtual (484-493), an SR pick requires roll x < srWeightTotal (prob srWeightTotal/wb); otherwise it validates a pledge (`pledgerOf[cand][day]==_safeOwnerOf(cand) && ownerSince<=freeze`, 488) and on failure burns a reject and re-rolls, up to 6/seat, voiding to NOT_SEATED. An owner (pledge() requires ownerOf==msg.sender, 296) who pledges many passes for day D then transfers them before the freeze makes all those copies invalid but still counted in wb. This (i) drives srWeightTotal/wb toward zero so SR-band hits become rare, and (ii) rapidly exhausts the SHARED MAX_VDRAW_ROLLS=256 budget — after which line 482 returns NOT_SEATED immediately for every remaining seat BEFORE even trying an SR pick, voiding SR autos too. Voided virtual seats still consume a draw iteration (total-- at 425) but paidRemaining is untouched, so paid winners are unaffected; the free 10% simply isn't seated and rolls forward to the vault. The line 112/130-131 comments overstate: the per-seat sub-cap bounds GAS, and vPool caps the free POOL at 10%, but neither prevents starvation of that 10% by an inflated sampling denominator + shared-budget exhaustion. Correctly low: pure griefing, non-profitable (redistributes to rollover, not to attacker), and requires owning a large share of the small pass supply (bar scales down when legit SR count is low).

**Fix:** Make SR autos immune to pledge-flooding: attempt an SR pick FIRST (roll over the srWeightTotal band independently of pledgeList and of the shared reject budget) before touching the pledge region, so an invalid-pledge flood cannot pre-empt the always-valid SR seats or drain the budget against them. Additionally, exclude transferred-away pledges from wb — e.g. track realized-valid pledge weight, or compact/prune pledgeList (and decrement its contribution) when pledgerOf no longer matches — so a pledge-then-transfer cannot inflate the sampling denominator. Consider giving the pledge region its own reject budget separate from SR.

**Resolution:** CLOSED (decision 2a + cascade fix) — `src/PackRegistry.sol`: Super Rare seats are picked first over their own band; and virtual seats are now independent (seat advanced on a void as well as a fill in both `_drawCore` and `_replayVirtual`), which removes a pre-existing cascade where one void ended the free block for the rest of the draw. The per-seat `q^6` void bound now holds empirically (measured on the `drawFrom` path); remaining pledge-region exposure documented as `SECURITY.md` §16.8; deeper rework (pruning invalid weight from `wb` / separate budget) deferred to external audit.

### [LOW] Reserve-then-abandon has no permissionless finalize path (protocol-wide brick)

- **Dimension:** mint-delegate
- **Location:** src/NFTCollection.sol:304-318 (reserveMint), 372-383 (publicMintExpired/launchBackstopExpired), 554-563 (finalizeLaunch); src/hooks/QpullTaxHook.sol:262

**Reasoning:** Reproduced from the code. reserveMint (304-318) mints up to RESERVE_CAP=25 real transferable NFTs to the treasury and requires mintStart==0 (line 306); it never stamps mintStart. Both permissionless-finalize triggers are gated on mintStart!=0: publicMintExpired() returns `mintStart != 0 && block.timestamp > publicMintClosesAt()` (373) and launchBackstopExpired() returns `mintStart != 0 && block.timestamp > mintStart + LAUNCH_BACKSTOP()` (382), so with mintStart==0 both are permanently false. finalizeLaunch (555) then requires msg.sender==owner() forever. revealRound is only assigned in finalizeLaunch (561), so rarityOf reverts on `require(revealRound != 0)` (527) forever, tokenURI serves sealed art forever, and QpullTaxHook.afterInitialize reverts MintStillOpen until nft.launched() (QpullTaxHook.sol:262), so no pool/swap can ever open. The pass-10 LAUNCH_BACKSTOP is measured from mintStart and therefore only rescues an owner who already STARTED the mint; the reserve step runs strictly before that clock, so the one state where real tokens already exist (totalMinted==qty, mintStart==0) has no backstop. The scenario (setRecipients -> reserveMint -> key loss before openAllowlistMint) is a genuine, unrecoverable brick with tokens already minted. Severity correctly LOW: it is real and unrecoverable but requires the narrow precondition of owner key loss in the exact window between reserveMint and openAllowlistMint; it is a gap in the very key-loss safety net (the backstop) rather than an attacker-triggerable flaw.

**Fix:** Arm the permissionless path on a reserve-based clock. In reserveMint, stamp a timestamp (e.g. reserveAt = block.timestamp) and have launchBackstopExpired() also return true when mintStart==0 && reserveAt!=0 && block.timestamp > reserveAt + LAUNCH_BACKSTOP(). Since finalizeLaunch and _requireMintable both key off launchBackstopExpired(), this lets a stranger finalize (reveal + enable the pool) a collection that minted a reserve but never opened, while never racing a live mint (mint paths are still gated by phase()/mintStart). Add a test covering reserveMint-then-never-open.

**Resolution:** CLOSED — `src/NFTCollection.sol` (`reserveMint` stamps `reserveAt`; `launchBackstopExpired()` also fires when `mintStart == 0 && reserveAt != 0 && block.timestamp > reserveAt + LAUNCH_BACKSTOP()`, so a stranger can finalize a reserved-only collection; once the mint starts only the `mintStart` clock counts). Operator rule recorded in `LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md` pinned gate H (open the allowlist mint within 30 days of `reserveMint`).

### [LOW] Auction soft-close relies on an unenforced invariant MAX_EXTENSION() < PUBLIC_MINT_WINDOW()

- **Dimension:** mint-delegate
- **Location:** src/NFTCollection.sol:416 (hardCap in _maybeExtendOverflow), 341/365 (fixed close), 90 (MAX_EXTENSION), 74 (PUBLIC_MINT_WINDOW)

**Reasoning:** Reproduced. _maybeExtendOverflow caps overflowEnd at hardCap = s + GTD_WINDOW() + OVERFLOW_WINDOW() + MAX_EXTENSION() (416), while phase() and publicMintClosesAt() fix the close at mintStart + GTD_WINDOW() + OVERFLOW_WINDOW() + PUBLIC_MINT_WINDOW() (341, 365). A non-empty PUBLIC window therefore requires MAX_EXTENSION() < PUBLIC_MINT_WINDOW(). If violated, for t in (close, overflowEnd) phase() returns ALLOWLIST (t < oe at line 338) but _requireMintable reverts via publicMintExpired() (401, since t > close), and phase() can never return PUBLIC (line 341 only returns PUBLIC when t <= close, and by then t >= oe is false), so the open no-proof window silently shrinks to zero. ### [INFO] lockRouting() + write-once adapters make any post-lock adapter/router defect permanently unfixable; lock currently runs inside the initial deploy tx before end-to-end pool verification

- **Dimension:** treasury-convert
- **Location:** src/Treasury.sol:132-141; script/Deploy.s.sol:270

**Reasoning:** Accurate as an info-level immutability observation; no bug. lockRouting() makes routing one-way: setAdapters (Treasury.sol:103) and setRouting (114) revert once routingLocked. Combined with write-once adapter bindings — QpullWethAdapter.setTreasury (103-108) and setPoolKey (81-96), QuotronRouterAdapter.setTreasury (53-58) — convert() is bound to those exact adapter contracts and the specific QPULL/WETH PoolKey forever after the lock. If either adapter, its bound PoolKey, or the external QUOTRON router later proves defective/deprecated, convert() is permanently bricked with no upgrade path — an intentional trust/immutability trade-off, called out in the code comments. The load-bearing detail the finding correctly surfaces: _wire calls lockRouting() at Deploy.s.sol:270 as part of the INITIAL deploy transaction, i.e. before the QPULL/WETH pool exists (it is created at go-live) and therefore before the QPULL/WETH leg can be fork-verified end-to-end against a live pool; the ConvertSplitFork test covers only the WETH->QUOTRON leg. So lockRouting()'s timing and pre-lock verification are genuinely load-bearing. Confirmed at info.

**Fix:** Move lockRouting() out of the initial deploy _wire and make it a separate, final go-live step executed only after BOTH adapters are fork-verified end-to-end against the live QPULL/WETH and QUOTRON pools (extend coverage beyond ConvertSplitFork's QUOTRON-only leg). Treat lockRouting() as the last verified-on-mainnet action, not part of the deploy transaction, so a bad adapter/PoolKey binding is caught before it is frozen.

**Resolution:** CLOSED — `lockRouting()` is removed from `script/Deploy.s.sol`'s `_wire` and is now the FINAL step (step 6) of `script/GoLiveMainnet.s.sol`, after the caps are armed, LP is seeded through the lock, and both adapter legs are fork-verified end-to-end (`LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md` §6b steps 4 to 6). The permanence of the lock itself stays an accepted immutability trade-off, `SECURITY.md` §16.6.
